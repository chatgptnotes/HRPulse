## Overtime Rule

User requested overtime calculation:

- Based on actual punch-out compared to assigned shift end time.
- Example shift: `09:00` to `18:00`.
- If punch-out is `20:15`, overtime is `2 hours 15 minutes`.
- Overtime only payable if overtime is more than 2 hours beyond shift end.
- If overtime is 2 hours or less, no overtime pay.
- If overtime exceeds 2 hours, the full overtime duration beyond shift end is recorded.
- Overtime pay per eligible day:
  - Per Day Salary = Monthly Salary / 30
  - Overtime Per Day = (Monthly Salary / 30) / 2

Implemented/worked on:

- HRPulse payroll service calculates overtime from actual `time_out` and employee `shift_end_time`.
- Fallback shift end time is `18:00` if employee shift end is missing.
- Overtime is counted only when employee works more than 120 minutes after shift end.
- Adamrit HR salary card was updated to show overtime hours and overtime pay from HRPulse.
- HRPulse ESS monthly endpoint was updated so Adamrit monthly summary can show overtime hours from HRPulse payroll detail.
