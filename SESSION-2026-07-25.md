## Overtime Logic

Confirmed/implemented expected overtime business rule direction.

Rule:

- Overtime is based on actual punch-out compared with assigned shift end time.
- Example shift: `09:00` to `18:00`.
- If punch-out is `20:15`, overtime is 2 hours 15 minutes.
- Overtime is payable only if employee worked more than 2 hours after shift end.
- If overtime is 2 hours or less, no overtime payment is generated.
- If overtime is more than 2 hours, the full overtime duration beyond shift end is payable.

Salary formula:

- Per Day Salary = Monthly Salary / 30
- Overtime Per Day = (Monthly Salary / 30) / 2

Adamrit display:

- Monthly summary shows overtime hours returned by HRPulse.
- Salary card shows overtime hours and overtime pay returned by HRPulse.
- Adamrit does not calculate overtime itself.

## Half-Day Logic

Confirmed/implemented expected half-day business rule direction.

Rule:

- If total working hours are less than 4 hours, the attendance status becomes Half Day.
- If employee works 4 hours or more, attendance follows normal full-day policy.

Payroll formula:

- Half Day Deduction = (Monthly Salary / 30) / 2
- Payable day for half day = 0.5

Adamrit display:

- Monthly summary shows half-day count.
- Salary card shows half days and half-day deduction.
- Attendance history can show Half Day status when returned by HRPulse.
