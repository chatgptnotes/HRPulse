## Missing Punch Policy

Previous behavior:

- Missing punch deducted half-day salary.

New behavior:

- Missing punch does not deduct salary.
- Missing punch still appears in attendance/payroll counts.
- If missing punches are more than 2, HRPulse sends a warning notification to Adamrit.
- 1 or 2 missing punches are shown as a count only and do not create Adamrit warning notifications.
- Individual daily missing-punch notification spam was removed.

Formula now:

```text
Missing Punch Deduction = INR 0
```

## Absent Deduction Policy

Payroll now shows absent salary impact clearly.

Current behavior:

- Absence affects salary through a visible absent deduction.
- Paid leaves cover absences first.
- Only unpaid absences deduct salary.

Example:

```text
Total Absences = 2
Paid Leaves = 2
Unpaid Absences = 0
Absent Deduction = INR 0
```

If unpaid absences exist:

```text
Absent Deduction = Unpaid Absences x Per Day Salary
```

The payroll detail screen now shows:

- Absences: total absent records.
- Paid Leaves: paid leaves used.
- Unpaid Absences: absences that actually deduct salary.
- Absent salary impact: amount deducted for unpaid absences.

## Department-Based Paid Leave And Sunday Policy

Implemented the requested department policy.

Per-day salary:

```text
Per Day Salary = Monthly Salary / 26
```

IT department:

- Sunday is weekly off.
- Sunday not attempted does not deduct salary.
- IT employees get 2 paid casual leaves per month.

Non-IT departments:

- Sunday is a working day.
- Sunday not attempted counts as absence.
- Non-IT employees get 4 paid leaves per month.

Paid leave application:

```text
Paid leaves cover absences first.
Remaining absences become unpaid absences.
Unpaid absences deduct salary.
```

Half-day deduction:

```text
Half-Day Deduction = Per Day Salary / 2
```

Late deduction:

```text
Every 3 late arrivals = 1 salary day deduction
Late Deduction = Late Deduction Days x Per Day Salary
```

Missing punch:

```text
Missing Punch Deduction = INR 0
```

## Nisha Salary Explanation Covered

Explained that salary is monthly, but deductions come from date-wise attendance totals.

After department policy, calculation pattern is:

```text
Gross Salary = Monthly Salary + Overtime Pay
Total Deductions = Absent Deduction + Half-Day Deduction + Late Deduction + Rule Deduction
Final Net Salary = Gross Salary - Total Deductions
```

For employees with salary INR 15,000:

```text
Per Day Salary = 15,000 / 26
Per Day Salary = INR 577 approximately
```

## Remaining Excel Upload Behavior

When attendance is uploaded in parts:

```text
Upload 1-25 attendance
26-30/31 appear as Not Attempted
Not Attempted counts as absence for temporary payroll
```

When remaining attendance is uploaded:

```text
Upload 26-30/31 attendance
HRPulse recalculates full month payroll
Real attendance replaces Not Attempted for those dates
Final salary becomes accurate for the full month
```
