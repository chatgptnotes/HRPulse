## Department-Based Leave and Weekly Off Rule

The user clarified different policies for IT and non-IT/hospital staff.

### IT Department

IT employees:
- Get weekly off on Sundays.
- Get 2 paid leaves per month.
- Sunday weekly offs are paid.
- If Sunday is shown as weekly off, it should not become unpaid absence.

Example:

```text
Department = IT
Sunday = Weekly Off
Paid leave quota = 2
```

### Non-IT / Hospital Staff

Hospital staff except IT:
- Do not get Sunday weekly off.
- They have to come on Sundays.
- They get 4 paid leaves per month.
- If they do not come on Sunday, that Sunday can be counted under their 4 paid leaves if available.
- If paid leaves are exhausted, the absence becomes unpaid and salary is deducted.

Example:

```text
Department = Reception / Nursing / General / Other hospital staff
Sunday absent = absence
Paid leave quota = 4
```

## Payroll Service Changes

File affected:

```text
backend/src/services/payrollService.ts
```

Implemented/confirmed behavior:

- Salary divisor is 30.
- Missing punch does not deduct salary.
- Missing punch only creates alert/notification.
- Late coming deduction rule:

```text
Every 3 late arrivals = 1 salary day deduction
```

- Half-day deduction:

```text
Half Day Deduction = (Monthly Salary / 30) / 2
```

- Overtime pay:

```text
Overtime Per Day = (Monthly Salary / 30) / 2
```

- Overtime applies only to employees manually marked as overtime eligible.
- Overtime only counts when employee works more than 2 hours beyond shift end.
- If overtime is 2 hours or less, no overtime payment is generated.
- If overtime is more than 2 hours, the full overtime duration is payable.

Example:

```text
Shift = 09:00 AM to 06:00 PM
Punch out = 08:15 PM
Overtime duration = 2 hours 15 minutes
Payable overtime = 2 hours 15 minutes
```

## Nisha / Himanshu Salary Explanation Discussed

The user showed examples where:

- Monthly salary = INR 15,000
- Per day salary = INR 500
- Present days were low
- Paid leaves were applied
- Remaining absent days became unpaid

Explained salary formula:

```text
Gross Salary = Monthly Salary + Overtime Pay + Rule Allowances
Total Deductions = Absent Deduction + Late Deduction + Half-Day Deduction + Rule Deductions + Other Deductions
Net Salary = Gross Salary - Total Deductions
```

For example:

```text
Monthly Salary = INR 15,000
Per Day Salary = INR 500
Unpaid Absences = 21
Absent Deduction = 21 x 500 = INR 10,500
Final Net Salary = 15,000 - 10,500 = INR 4,500
```

The user wanted this explanation visible in the UI, not only verbally.
