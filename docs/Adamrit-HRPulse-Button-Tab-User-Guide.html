Adamrit HRPulse Simple User Guide

Generated on: 25 July 2026

This guide explains every main tab, button, card, and small action added or used in HRPulse and Adamrit HR. It is written in simple language so HR team members and hospital staff can understand how the system works.

Private passwords, API keys, service-role keys, and secret tokens are not included in this document.

1. Two Applications

There are two separate applications:

  • HRPulse
  • Adamrit

They are connected, but they are not merged.

HRPulse is the master system for HR work.

Adamrit is the hospital system where employees already log in every day. Adamrit now shows an HR tile so employees can see their own HR information.

2. Main Rule

HRPulse does the calculation.

Adamrit only shows the result.

This means:

  • Attendance calculation happens in HRPulse.
  • Salary calculation happens in HRPulse.
  • Overtime calculation happens in HRPulse.
  • Half-day deduction happens in HRPulse.
  • Leave approval happens in HRPulse.
  • Notifications are generated by HRPulse.
  • Adamrit displays the final information to the employee.

3. HRPulse Main Tabs

3.1 Dispatcher Tab

Purpose:

The Dispatcher tab is used to upload and process biometric attendance Excel files.

What HR does here:

  • Upload daily attendance Excel.
  • Upload multiple days together.
  • Process attendance.
  • Save attendance records into HRPulse.
  • Trigger automatic attendance analysis.
  • Trigger automatic employee notifications.

Important:

If you upload data for 1 day, HRPulse analyzes that day.

If you upload data for 4 to 10 days, HRPulse analyzes every date separately.

If the sheet contains next month data, HRPulse puts those records into the correct next month.

Upload Attendance Button

What it does:

  • Opens file picker.
  • HR selects biometric Excel file.
  • HRPulse reads employee punch-in and punch-out data.
  • HRPulse detects date, month, and year.
  • HRPulse saves attendance records.
  • HRPulse starts alert generation after processing.

Process Attendance Button

What it does:

  • Starts attendance processing.
  • Converts raw Excel punch data into clean attendance records.
  • Finds first punch-in and last punch-out.
  • Calculates working hours.
  • Applies shift time.
  • Detects status such as Present, Absent, Half Day, Late, Missing Punch, Weekly Off, or Holiday Work.

Warnings After Upload

What it means:

If HRPulse shows a warning, it does not always mean upload failed.

Examples:

  • Multiple months found in one file.
  • Some employees could not be matched.
  • Some rows had missing data.
  • Some records were replaced because they already existed.

3.3 Employees Tab

Designation Field

What it does:

Shift End Time Field

What it does:

Status Field

What it does:

  • Inactive means employee should not be treated as active for HR operations.

What it does:

4. Adamrit Tablet View

Adamrit is the hospital management system.

Employees log in with their existing Adamrit username/password.

The HR tile is added inside Tablet View.

4.1 HR Tile

Purpose:

Opens the employee's HR self-service portal.

What happens when clicked:

Adamrit checks the logged-in employee.

Adamrit sends secure request to HRPulse.

HRPulse identifies matching employee.

HRPulse returns only that employee's data.

Adamrit displays it.

Important:

Employee cannot select another employee from the HR tile.

9. Salary Card

Purpose:

  • Net
  • Payable
  • Working
  • Half days

Total salary before deductions.

Total salary deductions.

  • Absence deduction
  • Half-day deduction

Final salary after deductions and additions.

Payable

Example:

Half Days

Number of half-day records.

Salary deduction because of half day.

Formula:

Half Day Deduction = (Monthly Salary / 30) / 2

Overtime Hours

Total overtime hours calculated by HRPulse.

Overtime Pay

Overtime payment calculated by HRPulse.

Formula:

Per Day Salary = Monthly Salary / 30

Overtime Per Day = (Monthly Salary / 30) / 2

15. Day-Wise Notification Examples

15.1 Late Arrival

Condition:

Employee punch-in is later than shift start time.

Example:

  • Shift start: 09:00
  • Punch in: 10:05

Notification:

Late arrival on 17 Jun 2026

Where it shows:

  • Adamrit HR notification bell
  • Adamrit notification drawer
  • Toast message if HR tile is open

15.2 Missing Punch-Out

Condition:

Employee has punch-in but no punch-out.

Notification:

Your attendance on 09 Jun 2026 has a missing punch-out. Please regularize it before payroll processing.

15.3 Missing Punch-In

Condition:

Employee has punch-out but no punch-in.

Notification:

Your attendance on 09 Jun 2026 has a missing punch-in. Please regularize it before payroll processing.

15.4 Absent

Condition:

No valid attendance record for a working day.

Notification:

Employee gets absence notification for that date.

15.5 Half Day

Condition:

Working hours are less than 4 hours.

Payroll effect:

Half day deduction is applied.

Notification:

Employee gets half-day alert for that date.

15.6 Overtime

Condition:

Employee works more than 2 hours after shift end.

Example:

  • Shift end: 06:00 PM
  • Punch out: 08:15 PM
  • Overtime: 2 hours 15 minutes

Notification:

Employee gets overtime notification for that date.

Salary effect:

Overtime pay is calculated by HRPulse.

15.7 Low Attendance Percentage

Condition:

Monthly attendance percentage falls below defined threshold.

Notification:

Employee gets warning in Adamrit notification bell.

This is a monthly pattern alert, not a single-day alert.

16. What Happens After Excel Upload

Simple flow:

HR uploads Excel in HRPulse Dispatcher.

HRPulse reads all attendance rows.

HRPulse identifies employee by employee id/code/email.

HRPulse identifies exact date from sheet.

HRPulse saves attendance record.

HRPulse calculates status.

HRPulse checks late, missing punch, absent, half day, overtime, etc.

HRPulse creates notification for each affected employee/date.

HRPulse pushes notification to Adamrit.

Employee opens Adamrit HR tile.

Employee sees notification in bell.

17. Why Employee May Not See Data

Possible reasons:

  • Employee email in HRPulse is missing.
  • Employee email in HRPulse is different from Adamrit login email.
  • HRPulse backend is not running.
  • Adamrit local server is not running.
  • Integration token is missing.
  • Notification webhook URL is wrong.
  • Supabase table is missing.
  • Employee has no attendance data for selected month.
  • Month selector is on wrong month.
  • Browser is logged in as different employee.

Before testing notifications:

  • Open HRPulse Employee Master.

20. Important Notes

  • Do not map employees by name.
  • Always use email or employee ID/code.
  • HRPulse must remain master.
  • Adamrit should not calculate salary.
  • Adamrit should not calculate attendance.
  • Adamrit should not approve leave.
  • Adamrit should only show the employee's own HR data.
  • Notification messages should be generated by HRPulse.
  • Day-wise messages should mention the exact attendance date.

21. Summary

HRPulse is the HR brain.

Adamrit is the employee screen.

HRPulse receives attendance Excel, calculates all HR rules, creates notifications, calculates salary, manages leave, and stores documents.

Adamrit lets the employee view their own HR information, submit leave, upload documents, download payslips, and read HRPulse notifications.

Every button and tab exists to keep HR work centralized in HRPulse while giving employees a simple self-service experience inside Adamrit.
